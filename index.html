01 About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Engineering at the University of South Carolina. My dissertation, “Hardware–Software Co-Design and Design-Space Exploration of Processor Architectures for Efficient GEMM,” spans the full stack — from RTL and on-chip interconnects to compiler backends and ML-driven performance modeling.

Previously I earned an M.S. in Computer Engineering from California State University, Fullerton and a B.Tech. in Electronics & Communication Engineering from IET Lucknow, and worked on RTL and DSP hardware at Pacific Microchip Corp. and HDLexpress.

02 Research Projects

Architecture · Compiler

RISCA

Configurable many-core VLIW RISC-V accelerator

A throughput-oriented accelerator for AI and GEMM workloads that integrates vector processing with a two-dimensional systolic interconnect. I co-designed the processor array, distributed scratchpads, streaming datapaths, and double-buffered memory hierarchy — together with a custom LLVM backend for instruction scheduling, VLIW packetization, software pipelining, and communication-aware kernels.

  • SystemVerilog
  • LLVM
  • RISC-V
  • Cycle-level sim
  • FPGA
ML for Systems · FPGA

N-TORC

Native Tensor Optimizer for Real-time Constraints

An ML-driven design-space exploration and resource-scheduling framework for FPGA-based scientific and real-time AI workloads. Analytical and learned models predict latency, throughput, and resource utilization, and an optimizer automatically selects hardware configurations that meet execution and resource constraints. Published at FPGA ’25 and FCCM ’25.

  • HLS
  • ILP / Optimization
  • Python
  • Xilinx ZCU104

Emerging Hardware

Memristive Crossbar Solver

Decomposition-based solver with FPGA acceleration

A decomposition-based approach to solving linear systems on memristive crossbar arrays, paired with an FPGA-accelerated hardware implementation for evaluation. Published at GLSVLSI ’25.
